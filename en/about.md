I'm Tom Carpanete, a fourth-year engineering student at ECE Paris, on the embedded
systems track. Before that I spent a semester on Erasmus exchange at the University
of Ruse, in Bulgaria.

What I enjoy is hardware that actually runs: a board, firmware on it, and a control
loop that holds. Most of what I publish here are personal projects — I write them up
as I go, dead ends included, because that's usually where the interesting part is.

## What I use

**Languages** — C, C++, Python, VHDL, SQL
**Embedded** — ESP32, ATmega328P, Arduino framework, SPI / I²C, PID control, MQTT
**CAD / EDA** — KiCad, Quartus, Proteus, SolidWorks
**Other** — Git, Grafana, Betaflight / ArduPilot
