I'm Tom Carpanete. I'm a fourth-year engineering student at ECE Paris, on the
embedded systems track. Before that, a semester on Erasmus exchange at the
University of Ruse, in Bulgaria.

What I enjoy is hardware that runs: a board, firmware on it, a control loop that
holds its setpoint. Most of the projects published here are personal ones, from an
FPV drone built part by part to PID control on an espresso machine. I document them
as I go, dead ends included: an abandoned approach often explains the problem better
than the fix that ended up working.

## What I use

- **Languages** — C, C++, Python, VHDL, SQL
- **Embedded** — ESP32, ATmega328P, Arduino framework, SPI / I²C, PID control, MQTT
- **CAD / EDA** — KiCad, Quartus, Proteus, SolidWorks
- **Other** — Git, Grafana, Betaflight / ArduPilot
